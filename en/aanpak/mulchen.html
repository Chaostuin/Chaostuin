Mulching

Cover your soil the way nature would: continuously, mixed, and airy

What is it?

Mulching means keeping your soil covered with organic material.

Main benefits

  • retains moisture
  • feeds soil life
  • suppresses weed growth
  • view all pros and cons

How do you use it?

  • always keep a stock on hand
  • don't put on the compost heap what can serve as mulch
  • mulch everywhere, except where you sow directly in the ground — there you temporarily remove the mulch
  • use a mix of plant material, preferably shredded
  • apply a layer up to 10 cm
  • top up regularly, all year round
  • don't smother your young plants

Nuance

Don't use mulch that mats together — keep the mixture airy.
