Layout

No rigid plots, but a living puzzle of plants, space and timing.

What is it?

You build up your garden layer by layer, instead of putting everything in rigid rows.

How does it work?

  • combinations make better use of space
  • soil stays more covered
  • harvest spreads out better over time

How do you apply it?

  • plan the large plants or groups first
  • then fill in with smaller species
  • combine plants in both space and time
  • don't block your steps with large plants
  • use the handy Vegetable Planner

Nuance / when

Chaos doesn't mean everything is placed at random. The structure isn't in straight lines, but in smart combinations.
