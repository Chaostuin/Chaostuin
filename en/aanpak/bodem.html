No-dig and soil building

Your soil isn't an empty medium. It's a living system that works for you — if you give it the chance.

What is it?

Your soil is a living system, not an empty medium.

Why does it work?

  • full of life and networks
  • builds its own structure
  • retains water and nutrients
  • repairs itself if you don't disturb it

How do you work with it?

  • stop digging or turning it over
  • keep the soil covered at all times (mulch)
  • add organic material on top
  • don't walk on your growing beds

Nuance / when

In the beginning it feels slower. But beneath the surface, the real work begins.
