The Adventures of Lientje

Lientje's exciting adventures in ecological gardening

What is it about?

When Lientje throws an apple core onto the compost heap, she discovers a hidden world beneath her feet.

What does she discover?

  • compost is alive
  • worms and bacteria work together
  • air and balance matter
  • the wrong junk makes everything sick

Who is Grommel?

A waste monster that grows from the wrong things ending up in the compost, like plastic, batteries and fake cardboard.

Why is this little book special?

It explains composting simply through adventure, humor and imagination, for kids and adults alike.
